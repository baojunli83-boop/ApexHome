WHO WE ARE

Residential construction rooted in Greater Seattle.

Seattle Apex Home Construction, LLC has residential construction experience in the Greater Seattle area, including single-family homes and townhouse developments.

The company was formerly known as Seattle ALD Investment LLC and was renamed in 2024. Seattle Apex holds a general contractor license for residential construction in the Greater Seattle area.
